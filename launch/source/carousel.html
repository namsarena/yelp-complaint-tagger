How I built it

Five steps, one day, $0.

01

Write the rules

8 labels plus rules: praise isn’t a complaint; slow isn’t rude.

02

Label with a free LLM

03

Beat a baseline

Simple keyword model first, as the score to beat.

04

Fine-tune DistilBERT

66M parameters, weighted for rare labels, ~15 min on a free GPU.

What I learned · what’s next

The model copies its teacher.

  • Labels need written rules, or even humans disagree.
  • Rare complaints need extra weight, or the model ignores them.
  • AI-made labels carry the AI’s habits. Check them by hand.

Next:

  • A bigger human-checked test set
  • Add happy and neutral reviews
  • More rare examples
  • Show the evidence
  • A weekly dashboard
